MediaHub · design specimen · both themes follow the phone

Bijuli · Volt on Ink

One loud colour, Volt lime, on a deep ultraviolet ink. In the light theme the canvas becomes ivory paper, the reading colour becomes Ultraviolet ink, and Volt turns into a highlighter "sticker" with an ink edge. The Ink Dock (mini player + nav) and the playback stage stay ink in both themes.

Type: Unbounded for app mastheads and section heads, Anek Devanagari Wide for anything a song, video or person is called (Latin and Nepali in one voice), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next + Mukta for every line people read, DM Mono for times, sizes and kickers. Fonts load from raw.githubusercontent.com; the app bundles the same files.
